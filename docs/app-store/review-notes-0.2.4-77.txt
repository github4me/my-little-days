VERSION 0.2.4 (BUILD 77)
Records > Sleep guidance estimates only remaining sleep and windows to BEGIN settling, from the current time to the next local midnight. It first considers the previous seven complete local dates (excluding today), then today's observations and exact age in days. Broad WHO total-sleep and Raising Children Network tiredness references provide soft context. No browsing, on-device prediction, diagnosis, treatment or guaranteed sleep times. Insufficient evidence can return no estimate.

AI SERVICE AND STORAGE
Our authenticated server sends OpenAI age in days, timezone/current time, sleep/feed times/types/milk amounts for seven previous complete local dates and today, plus the earlier same-day AI estimate. Names, full birth dates, notes, photos and account/family identifiers are excluded from the provider input.
Server tasks/results allow generation to continue independently of the app. Ordinary task input clears at task termination; ordinary results expire at local midnight. Separately, restricted diagnostic capture retains provider request/response bodies, timing/errors and task correlation for seven days, with periodic cleanup. Credentials are excluded. This includes successful and failed exchanges. Turning AI off stops future sending and requests task/result/diagnostic cleanup once connected; it cannot recall data already sent. The latest backend schema correction is deployed. The public and in-app retention wording has not been updated to describe this separate full-exchange diagnostic capture.
