VERSION 0.2.5 (BUILD 78)

This is a connection-recovery update to the publicly available 0.2.4 (77). Refreshing account status now also rechecks AI availability after verified session recovery. Previously enabled AI controls remain visible during a temporary same-account disconnection, with Reconnect AI and local Turn off AI available. Reconnection resumes the same durable server job rather than submitting a duplicate paid generation. Cached presentation never grants permission to transmit data. Sign-in, separate AI consent, membership validation and account/profile isolation remain enforced.

AI is optional. The app sends age in days, timezone/current time, seven complete prior local dates plus today's sleep/feed records and an earlier same-day AI plan when enabled. Names, full dates of birth, photos and notes are excluded from AI requests. The server uses OpenAI to estimate remaining sleep and settling-start periods until local midnight; stored sleep remains authoritative. Suggestions are uncertain routine aids, not diagnosis or a proven optimal bedtime. Recording still works without AI. Generation continues server-side while the app is backgrounded; returning reconnects to the saved task.

The production server currently has restricted diagnostics capturing complete minimized provider requests and successful/failed responses for seven days, with scheduled expiry cleanup. The public and in-app retention wording has not been updated and still describes more limited diagnostics/request clearing. This known discrepancy remains unresolved; we are disclosing it explicitly here rather than presenting the existing wording as accurate.

Reviewer access: recording and historical charts work without an account. Optional AI requires ordinary sign-in using the reviewer's own email and the email one-time code. No fixed shared demo password exists; no review-only access bypass is implemented. The AI consent link remains available beside its enable/disable control. If testing AI, add a birth date and sleep/feed entries for the current local date and recent days; sparse evidence may return no suggestions. Turn off AI stops future sharing and requests server-side cancellation/clearing when connected.

The full-day sleep clock, Watch, widget, optional StoreKit coffee purchases, family sharing and account deletion flows are unchanged. No subscription or purchase is required for AI. Existing approved purchase products are not resubmitted. Connection recovery was regression-tested in automated app/controller/browser fixtures; no new physical iPhone/Watch acceptance is claimed for this candidate.
